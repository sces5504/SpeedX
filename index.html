HOW TO PLAY

earn reputation · climb the blacklist · take their cars

01

STREET RACES

Racers cruise these roads at night. Get close to one and flash your lights (F) — or flash back when one flashes you. Hazards on, 3-2-1, and the first to the green gate wins. Any road will do: the map and the arrows on the road show the shortest way. A win pays money and reputation; a faster rival pays more.
02

THE BLACKLIST

The drivers who run this place. Each one asks for a certain reputation before they will race you. Open the Blacklist from the pause menu to challenge them. Beat a driver for the first time and their car is yours. The goal: number one.
03

ACTIVITIES

Speed cameras — drive past one as fast as you dare.
Time attack — stop by the orange cone at any stop and press E: reach the gate before the time runs out. Near misses give time back, crashes cost time.
Both give one to three stars. Each star pays once, in reputation and money.
04

GARAGE AND DEALER

Stop in front of a garage door and press E: performance parts, engine swaps, body parts and paint, your other cars, and fast travel. The dealership sells cars. Parts and cars cost money — races and stars earn it.
05

DRIVING

W A S D drive. SPACE is the handbrake: tap it while steering into a bend and the tail steps out — steer the slide with A / D, hold it with the throttle, steer straight to end it. C changes the camera, L the lights, BACKSPACE puts the car back on the road.
06

THE MAP AND REPLAYS

M opens the map: click a stop to travel there, click a road to be shown the way. After a race, a time attack or a speed camera, press R to watch it again as a film. ESC pauses: rain, time of night, picture filter, audio.
