# prelude — Honda Prelude Si, US (BB2), 1993 — street-tuned look

Roster car 02. The user asked for "帥一點的版本": built as a tuned car (lowered ~3 cm, slim skirts,
front lip, body-colour pedestal wing like the Si VTEC's). Performance is the stock Si's.

## Measured off the photos
- Side photo is wide-angle (both ends foreshortened ~25 %): t positions between the axles taken
  directly, the overhangs scaled up to the real length. Front overhang ~0.88-0.91 m.
- Windscreen foot t 0.335, roof 0.463-0.665, long wrap-round rear glass down to the deck at 0.905,
  short high deck (~0.95 m), quarter window sweeping up into the rear glass.
- Front: slim headlamps right under the bonnet edge with a small louvred vent at their inner end,
  long amber lamp low in the bumper, thin black lower slot.
- Rear: wedge tail lamps at the corners (sloped inner edge, apex at the top outer corner, wrapping
  onto the side), amber band along their bottom; plate between them; twin tips on one side.

## Deliberate differences (fictional car)
No badge; vents plain; lamps simplified to two round units each.
