# sx240 — Nissan 240SX coupe, US (S13), 1991

Roster car 03. The US coupe: the Silvia S13 coupe body with the 180SX's sloping nose and pop-up lamps.
`s13` (the JDM Silvia K's) was retired from the game on 2026-11-18; its folder is kept in reference/retired-cars/.

## How it was made
- Cabin, doors, windows and tail copied from `s13` (accepted); lowered 2 cm (street look).
- Overhangs kept at the `s13`'s (4.47 m long, not the spec's 4.52): the user does not want noses and
  tails stretched past what the photos show.
- 240SX details: black rub strip round the bumpers and along the doors, US amber / red side markers.
- New nose: low wedge with the pop-up lids at its leading edge, smooth face (no grille), bumper with a
  black rub strip, clear corner lamps, amber side markers and a slot intake low in the middle.
- Factory-style low boot spoiler; slim skirts.

## Deliberate differences
No badges or "240SX" lettering.
